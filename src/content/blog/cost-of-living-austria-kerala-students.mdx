# Cost of Living in Austria for Kerala Students (2026 Guide)

Studying in Austria offers exceptional value compared to other Western European destinations. While public university tuition is fixed by law at **€726.72 per semester (~₹70,000 INR)**, understanding your monthly living budget in cities like **Vienna, Graz, Linz, or Innsbruck** is crucial for financial planning.

## 1. Student Accommodation (OEAD vs Private)

Housing is your largest recurring expense in Austria. International students holding an Austrian Student Residence Permit (*Aufenthaltsbewilligung Studierende*) usually book through official student residence organizations:

- **OEAD Housing**: High quality, furnished single or shared apartments near major university campuses. Rent includes heating, electricity, high-speed Wi-Fi, and laundry facilities.
- **Wihast Dorms**: Popular budget student halls in Vienna and Wiener Neustadt starting from **€320/month**.

> **Important Note for Visa Filing:** The Austrian Embassy in New Delhi requires a **legal rental contract** before issuing your student visa. FlyAustria locks in your OEAD/Wihast contract directly from Kochi.

## 2. Part-Time Work Rights & Earnings in Austria

Can international students cover their living costs by working part-time? **Yes!**

- **Permitted Hours:** Under Austrian immigration law, international students can work **20 hours per week** during university semesters, and **unlimited full-time hours** during winter/summer breaks.
- **Average Hourly Wage:** €12 to €16 per hour for student roles in IT, tutoring, logistics, hospitality, or research assistantships.
- **Monthly Earnings:** Working 20 hours/week at €13/hour yields approximately **€1,040 per month**, which comfortably covers all living expenses!

---

## 3. No Blocked Account Required!

Unlike Germany, where students must deposit over €11,904 into a locked *Sperrkonto* account every year, Austria accepts proof of funds in a **regular Indian savings bank account** held in your or your parents' name.
