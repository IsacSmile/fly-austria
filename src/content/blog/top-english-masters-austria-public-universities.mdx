# Top English-Taught Master's Degrees in Austrian Public Universities

Many Indian students assume German language proficiency (C1/B2) is mandatory to study in Austria. While German is helpful for daily life, **dozens of world-class Master’s degree programs in Austrian public universities are taught 100% in English!**

---

## Top English-Taught Master's Degrees

### 1. University of Vienna (Universität Wien)
- **M.Sc. Data Science**
- **M.Sc. Molecular Biology**
- **M.Sc. Business Analytics**
- **M.Sc. Economics**
- **Tuition:** €726.72 / semester

### 2. TU Wien (Vienna University of Technology)
- **M.Sc. Software Engineering & Internet Computing**
- **M.Sc. Biomedical Engineering**
- **M.Sc. Renewable Energy Systems**
- **M.Sc. Computer Science**
- **Tuition:** €726.72 / semester

### 3. TU Graz (Graz University of Technology)
- **M.Sc. Computer Science**
- **M.Sc. Software Development & Knowledge Management**
- **M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence & Robotics**
- **M.Sc. Biomedical Engineering**
- **Tuition:** €726.72 / semester
