# Austrian Student Residence Permit Visa Checklist 2026

Filing your Austrian Student Residence Permit (*Aufenthaltsbewilligung Studierende*) at VFS / Austrian Embassy New Delhi requires strict compliance with document authentication standards.

Here is the exact document checklist used by FlyAustria for 98%+ visa success.

---

## Required Document Stack

### 1. Official Application Form
- Duly completed and signed **Aufenthaltsbewilligung Studierende** form.
- Two biometric passport photos (35x45mm, light grey background).

### 2. University Admission Proof
- Original **Admission Letter (*Zulassungsbescheid*)** or preliminary confirmation from an Austrian public university (University of Vienna, TU Graz, TU Wien, JKU Linz).

### 3. Proof of Accommodation in Austria
- Confirmed rental contract (*Mietvertrag*) from OEAD Housing, Wihast, or private student dormitories for at least 6 months.

### 4. MEA Apostille Legalized Qualification Documents
- Bachelor's Degree Certificate and semester mark sheets attesting Kerala university credentials.
- Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) issued by Passport Office with MEA Apostille sticker.
- Birth Certificate in English with MEA Apostille sticker.

### 5. Financial Proof of Funds
- Bank statement showing minimum balance of ~**€11,000** for students aged under 24, or ~**€14,000** for students aged 24 and above.
- Bank balance can be maintained in an Indian savings account with a formal letter of sponsorship from parents.
